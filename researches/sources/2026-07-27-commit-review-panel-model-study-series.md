## Why this series was promoted to canon

A coordination conflict happened because the entire series — including the S3 probe
that corroborates the complexity-over-raise finding — lived **only under `tmp/`** and
was undiscoverable. A researcher searching for "the panel probe" could not find it.
The series is promoted wholesale to `researches/sources/` so it is **citable canon,
not tmp-only**, and so the S3 probe's over-raise corroboration and its
upstream/downstream memos remain resolvable after the `tmp/` originals evaporate.
